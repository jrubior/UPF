\textbf{Question 1}

(a) Define an efficient estimator and explain why this is an important
property and how it relates to the mean squared error of an estimator.

(b) Provide an expression for the bias of an estimator. How is the Mean
Squared Error (MSE)\ of an estimator related to its bias?

(c) State (without proof) the Gauss Markov and Cram\'{e}r Rao theorems, and
explain the differences between the two.

\bigskip

\textbf{Question 2}

Consider the linear regression model $y=X\beta +\varepsilon ,$ where $y$ is
an $n\times 1$ vector of dependent variables, $X$ is an $n\times k$ matrix
of regressors of rank $k$, $\beta $ is a $k\times 1$ vector of unknown
parameters, and $\varepsilon $ is an $n\times 1$ vector of unobserved random
variables. The standard assumptions provided in the lecture are assumed to
hold, particularly $\mathbb{E}\left[ \varepsilon |X\right] =0$ and $\mathbb{E%
}\left[ \varepsilon \varepsilon ^{\prime }|X\right] =\sigma ^{2}I_{n}.$

(a) Derive the ordinary least squares OLS estimator for $\beta .$

(b) Show that the OLS\ estimator $\hat{\beta}_{n}^{OLS}$ is unbiased,
carefully stating the assumptions you are using.

(c) Compute the variance of $\hat{\beta}_{n}^{OLS}.$

(d) Show that $\hat{\beta}_{n}^{OLS}$ is consistent, carefully stating the
assumptions you are using.

(e) Derive the asymptotic distribution of $\hat{\beta}_{n}^{OLS},$ carefully
stating the assumptions you are using$.$

(f) Explain how your answer in (e) can be used to conduct hypothesis testing
or construct confidence intervals for the unknown $\beta .$

\bigskip

\textbf{Question 3 }

Consider the Gaussian linear model $y=X\beta +\varepsilon ,$ where $\beta
\in \mathbb{R}^{K}$ and $\varepsilon |X\sim \mathcal{N}\left( 0,\sigma
^{2}\Omega \right) $ for an unknown scalar $\sigma ^{2}$ and some \textbf{%
known} $n\times n$ positive definite matrix $\Omega $.

(a) Write down the log-likelihood function of the sample.

HINT: Recall the definition of a Normal density of an $m\times 1$ vector $%
z\sim \mathcal{N}\left( \mu ,\Sigma \right) $: $\ $%
\begin{equation*}
f\left( z;\mu ,\Sigma \right) =\left( 2\pi \right) ^{-m/2}\det \left( \Sigma
\right) ^{-1/2}\exp \left\{ -\frac{1}{2}\left( z-\mu \right) ^{\prime
}\Sigma ^{-1}\left( z-\mu \right) \right\} .
\end{equation*}

(b) Show that the maximum likelihood estimator $\hat{\beta}^{_{MLE}}$ of $%
\beta $ coincides with the generalised least squares estimator $\hat{\beta}%
^{_{GLS}}$ of $\beta $.

(c) Show that $\hat{\beta}^{_{MLE}}$ is unbiased and calculate its variance
conditional on $X$.

(d) Find the maximum likelihood estimator for $\sigma ^{2}$.

(e) Compute the Fisher Information matrix of $[\beta ,\sigma ^{2}]$
conditional on $X$.

(f) Does the $\hat{\beta}^{_{MLE}}$ achieves the Cramer Rao Lower bound
[conditional on $X$]? How does this relate to the Gauss Markov theorem for
the GLS estimator?
