\textbf{Question 1}

(a) Provide a definition for an efficient estimator.

(b) State (without proof) the Gauss Markov and Cram\'{e}r Rao theorems, and
explain the differences between the two.

(c) Provide an expression for the bias of an estimator. How is the Mean
Squared Error (MSE)\ of an estimator related to its bias?

\bigskip

\textbf{Question 2}

Consider the linear regression model $y=X\beta +\varepsilon ,$ where $y$ is
an $n\times 1$ vector of dependent variables, $X$ is an $n\times k$ matrix
of regressors of rank $k$, $\beta $ is a $k\times 1$ vector of unknown
parameters, and $\varepsilon $ is an $n\times 1$ vector of unobserved random
variables. The standard assumptions provided in the lecture are assumed to
hold, particularly $\mathbb{E}\left[ \varepsilon |X\right] =0$ and $\mathbb{E%
}\left[ \varepsilon \varepsilon ^{\prime }|X\right] =\sigma ^{2}I_{n}.$

(a) Derive the ordinary least squares OLS estimator for $\beta .$

(b) Show that the OLS\ estimator $\hat{\beta}_{n}^{OLS}$ is unbiased,
carefully stating the assumptions you are using.

(c) Compute the variance of $\hat{\beta}_{n}^{OLS}.$

(d) Show that $\hat{\beta}_{n}^{OLS}$ is consistent, carefully stating the
assumptions you are using.

(e) Derive the asymptotic distribution of $\hat{\beta}_{n}^{OLS},$ carefully
stating the assumptions you are using$.$

(f) Explain how your answer in (e) can be used to conduct hypothesis testing
or construct confidence intervals for the unknown $\beta .$

\bigskip

\textbf{Question 3 }

Consider the linear regression model 
\begin{equation*}
y=X\beta +\varepsilon
\end{equation*}%
where $y$ is an $n\times 1$ vector of dependent variables, $X$ is an $%
n\times k$ matrix of stochastic regressors of rank $k$, $\varepsilon |X$ are 
$\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma ^{2}I_{n})$ random variables, and $\beta $ is a $%
k\times 1$ vector of unknown parameters$.$

(a) Set up the log likelihood function and derive the ML estimators $%
\widehat{\beta }^{ML}$ and $\widehat{\sigma }^{2,ML}.$

(b) Show that $\widehat{\sigma }^{2,ML}$ is biased and propose a bias
corrected estimator for $\sigma ^{2}.$

(c) Show that $\widehat{\sigma }^{2,ML}$ is asymptotically unbiased, and
consistent.

(d) Derive the distribution of $\widehat{\beta }^{ML}|X$ and explain how it
can be used for testing. How does inference in this setup differ from the
setup in question 2 (f)?

(e) Compute the Fisher information matrix for the $(k+1)\times 1$ vector of
parameters $[\beta ,\sigma ^{2}].$

(f) Are $\widehat{\beta }^{ML}$ and $\widehat{\sigma }^{2,ML}$ efficient?
