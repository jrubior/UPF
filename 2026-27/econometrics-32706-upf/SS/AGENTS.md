# Slice sampling slide editing instructions

Use capital letters for random variables and lowercase letters for their realized values. Write distributions with capital random variables and explicit conditioning values, for example `H \mid X=x \sim \operatorname{Unif}(0,q(x))`. Keep density arguments lowercase, as in `p(x,h)` and `p_X(x)`. In algorithms, distinguish random draws from their realized values, then use the realized values in later calculations. Use a capital Latin name such as `V` for an auxiliary random vector and lowercase `v` for its realization.

Do not use `\qquad` as a visual spacer between related mathematical statements. Connect them with a comma or the word “and”, and use prose when that makes the relationship clearer. Keep equations readable and rebuild the PDF after editing.
